laele-cli 

        $ laele -mode random
        
A terminal that speaks Bahian: “cada comando vem com uma gíria de Salvador, viu!” laele adds Salvador slang to your shell commands, placed before, in the middle of or after the command (or at random), with an option to show what each word means. The real thing is a Go terminal tool, and the website is a TypeScript imitation with a fake in-terminal shell you can try right in the browser, with a little capoeira, Pelourinho and acarajé culture on the side.

        
GoTypeScript

      
      
        
        
PendangaMine 

        
A web viewer that renders the Minecraft Bedrock world "Pendanga" straight from the real world geometry, in real time, at any coordinates, from the cherry-blossom pagoda to the farthest chunk. Switch between Classic and Beautiful graphics modes (shaded sunlight, soft lighting), save up to 50 places, share them on WhatsApp, and watch animated mobs that follow basic Minecraft rules.

        
Web

      
      

        
        
Crochet Compiler 

        
A DSL for writing crochet patterns as plain text, inspired by Lilypond and Mermaid. The text compiles into a standard symbol chart, then into a 3D model of the finished piece. Planned stack: Ruby for the language and parser, TypeScript with Three.js for rendering.

CapiNada Responde 

        
A sports-events app for Brazil covering triathlon, aquathlon, swimming and running, with a RAG assistant that answers questions about events. It stores its own downloadable copies of result PDFs and uses the Capivaras do Lago brand (capybara mascot, Bricolage Grotesque and Hanken Grotesk fonts). Built on a Rails API and Expo — web, iOS and Android from one codebase — hosted on Railway.

        
RailsExpo

      

      

        
        
Capi News 

        
Turns a group chat into a blog. Upload the exported conversation and it groups the messages into events (trainings, meetups, races), then asks Claude to write a short post for each one, plus a weekly summary of who took part. Drafts stick to what was said in the chat, phone numbers never reach the AI, the public site shows first names only, and photos are resized with location data stripped. Nothing goes online on its own: an admin approves, edits, regenerates or discards each draft. Re-uploads don't duplicate messages, and posts can also be written by hand in Markdown.

        
Claude

      

      

        
Outcrops Odyssey 

        
An open-world game where you play a field geologist exploring an endless, procedurally generated landscape. Each day you take the truck to a drop point, break rock outcrops for points, XP and collectible cards, and must get back to a road before sunset or it's game over. Built in Rust (ported from DragonRuby), it runs on desktop and in the browser, with mobile touch controls.

        
Rust

      

      

        
Outcrop Explorer 

        
A map app for field geologists to save GPS paths to outcrops and points of interest, with each path paired with a walkthrough video of the route (MVP). Projects can be private or public, videos are served via S3 presigned URLs, and share links give access to a location. Accounts use email-confirmed signup with optional 2FA. Built with a Rails backend, a Flutter mobile app and a Google-Maps-style landing page using Stimulus, starting with a GPX importer, then share links, then the UI, with seed data near Chapada dos Veadeiros, Brazil.

        
RailsFlutter
