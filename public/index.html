About

Who I am and what I've been building.

I'm a Software Engineer based in Brasília, Brazil, currently building with Ruby on Rails at AssureSoft, working on systems for the behavioral health industry.

Before that, I spent time at Origem Motos, Brazil's first electric-motorcycle maker, where I worked on Change Data Capture pipelines — Kafka, RabbitMQ, ETL tooling, Elasticsearch, and GraphQL.

AI-assisted development is now part of how I build — using tools like Claude and Cursor day to day, and working with agentic systems, RAG pipelines, and LLM tooling to ship faster without cutting corners on quality.

Outside of backend work, I'm into martial arts (Taekwondo, Boxing, Kickboxing, Muay Thai), open-water swimming, crochet, and live coding with Sonic Pi.

Music is a big part of my life too — I'm a clarinetist, and that same discipline of practice and timing carries over into how I approach engineering work.
